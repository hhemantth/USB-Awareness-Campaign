Think Before You Plug

You just ran a simulated ransomware payload from a USB external storage device. This was part of a security awareness campaign designed to highlight the risks of unknown USB storage devices and encourage safe practices.

  • Never plug in unknown external USB storage devices
  • Report suspicious external storage devices found in GF premises to EHS security Team
  • Follow IT security policies and instructions provided from GFShield trainings

Stay vigilant . Stay secure. Your awareness is your first line of defense.
